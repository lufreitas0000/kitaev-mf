\section{Conclusion and Physical Outlook}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this work, we have established a rigorous, mathematically unified Majorana parton mean-field framework for the extended Kitaev honeycomb model. By treating the self-consistent equations as a geometric fixed-point problem on the orthogonal Grassmannian manifold bundle, we resolved key computational and physical bottlenecks that have long constrained the field.

\subsection{Summary of Principal Discoveries}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Algebraic Invariance and Neutrality:} We proved the Flat-Band Gauge Neutrality Theorem, showing that local gauge Lagrange multipliers vanish identically ($\boldsymbol{\lambda}^* \equiv \mathbf{0}$) in the pure Kitaev limit due to destructive interference among flat localized bands. Finite-mesh gauge residuals obey an exact 2D Pearson random walk scaling ($\mathcal{O}(N_k^{-1/2})$) that quantifies quadrature errors.
\item \textbf{Field Multistability and Empirical Weak-Field Scaling:} Under magnetic fields along $[111]$, we identified a sharp first-order transition at $h_c^* / |K| = 0.3325 \pm 0.0005$ with an energy hysteresis barrier $\Delta E = 0.057 |K|$ and magnetization jump $\Delta M = 0.412$. In the weak-field topological regime, $C_3$ and $\mathcal{T}$-odd invariants mandate cubic gap scaling $\Delta_K \propto h^3$, while finite-mesh numerical fits yield $\Delta_K^{\mathrm{MF}} = (2.5921 \pm 0.0034) h^3/|K|^2$ and $\|\boldsymbol{\lambda}^*\|_2 \approx \sqrt{5} h$; closed-form analytical evaluation (\texttt{IDEA-001}) and full-$Q$ Feshbach--Schur resolvent comparisons (\texttt{K-019-A}) remain open analytical problems.
\item \textbf{Grassmannian Solver Pareto Frontiers:} Benchmarking across $N_{\mathrm{trials}} = 30$ independent symmetric random seeds established that Full Broyden and Joint DIIS form the optimal Pareto efficiency frontier ($R_{90} = 0.350\text{--}0.500$, $K_{50} = 27\text{--}34$), outperforming Picard iteration by $35\text{--}50\times$ in basin radius and $4.26\times$ in time-to-solution, while Damped L-Broyden ($\alpha = 0.5$) and SPD-floored Lehmann dual preconditioning stabilize stiff real-space and critical regimes.
\item \textbf{Supercell Scale Parity:} Generalized honeycomb supercells up to $n_c = 32$ sublattices demonstrated machine-precision intensive energy parity ($|E_{\mathrm{site}}(n_c) - E_{\mathrm{site}}(2)| \le 5.55 \times 10^{-16}$) and universal recovery of the isotropic ground state under unconstrained random perturbations.
\item \textbf{Eigenvector-Free Real-Space Purification and Plaquette Pfaffians:} Real skew-symmetric representations halved memory ($2.0\times$) alongside real orthogonal \texttt{canonical\_skew\_schur} reduction. Newton-Schulz and Kenney--Laub Pad\'e $[3/2]$ matrix sign purification $\Omega = \ii \operatorname{sgn}(\ii \mathbf{A})$ bypassed eigensolver deflation bottlenecks and $3 \mid L$ Dirac resonance squaring ($114.64\text{ GFLOP/s}$ sustained CPU throughput at $4 \times 10^{-15}$ accuracy), while Parlett--Reid 12-Majorana Pfaffians track exact Gaussian plaquette fluxes $\mathcal{W}_p$.
\end{enumerate}
