\section{Conclusion and Physical Outlook}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this work, we have established a rigorous, mathematically unified Majorana parton mean-field framework for the extended Kitaev honeycomb model. By treating the self-consistent equations as a geometric fixed-point problem on the orthogonal Grassmannian manifold bundle, we resolved key computational and physical bottlenecks that have long constrained the field.

\subsection{Summary of Principal Discoveries}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Algebraic Invariance and Neutrality:} We proved the Flat-Band Gauge Neutrality Theorem, showing that local gauge Lagrange multipliers vanish identically ($\boldsymbol{\lambda}^* \equiv \mathbf{0}$) in the pure Kitaev limit due to destructive interference among flat localized bands. Finite-mesh gauge residuals obey an exact 2D Pearson random walk scaling ($\mathcal{O}(N_k^{-1/2})$) that quantifies quadrature errors.
\item \textbf{Field Multistability and Response Laws:} Under magnetic fields along $[111]$, we identified a sharp first-order phase transition at $h_c^* / |K| = 0.3325 \pm 0.0005$ with an energy hysteresis barrier $\Delta E = 0.057 |K|$ and magnetization jump $\Delta M = 0.412$. In the weak-field topological regime, the Dirac mass opens with a cubic power law $\Delta_K = 2.592 h^3$ governed by $C_3$ and $\mathcal{T}$-odd invariants, while the Lagrange multiplier vector satisfies the universal scaling $\|\boldsymbol{\lambda}^*\| = \sqrt{5} h$.
\item \textbf{Grassmannian Solver Pareto Frontiers:} Benchmarking across $N_{\mathrm{trials}} = 30$ independent symmetric random seeds established that Full Broyden and Joint DIIS form the optimal Pareto efficiency frontier ($R_{90} = 0.350\text{--}0.500$, $K_{50} = 27\text{--}34$), outperforming Picard iteration by $35\text{--}50\times$ in basin radius and $4.26\times$ in time-to-solution, while Damped L-Broyden ($\alpha = 0.5$) stabilizes high-dimensional real-space manifolds ($D \sim 10^4$).
\item \textbf{Supercell Scale Parity:} Generalized honeycomb supercells up to $n_c = 32$ sublattices demonstrated machine-precision intensive energy parity ($|E_{\mathrm{site}}(n_c) - E_{\mathrm{site}}(2)| \le 5.55 \times 10^{-16}$) and universal recovery of the isotropic ground state under unconstrained random perturbations.
\item \textbf{Eigenvector-Free Real-Space Purification:} By eliminating complex Hermitian arithmetic, real skew-symmetric representations halved memory ($2.0\times$). Newton-Schulz matrix sign purification $\Omega = \ii \operatorname{sgn}(\ii \mathbf{A})$ bypassed eigensolver deflation bottlenecks and $3 \mid L$ Dirac resonance squaring, operating at $114.64\text{ GFLOP/s}$ sustained throughput on host CPU hardware with $4 \times 10^{-15}$ machine-precision accuracy.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Future Horizons and Multi-Phase Research Roadmap}
Having rigorously quantified all solver nuances, failure modes, and hardware resource scaling in Phase~1 on the pure ferromagnetic Kitaev model ($K = -1.0$) under a $[111]$ field, our research roadmap proceeds through dedicated model-family and physics phases:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Phase 2 (FM Kitaev $[111]$ Physics Production: Ground State, Vortices, and Thermodynamics):} Deploying the certified optimal solvers directly in the $K = -1.0$, $\mathbf{h} \parallel [111]$ regime on real-space clusters (with Bloch comparison as reality checks) to map: (i) the $T=0$ bulk ground state (Chern number, spectral weight, susceptibilities, and Gutzwiller/SDP variational bounds), (ii) $T=0$ states with isolated pairs of $\mathbb{Z}_2$ vortices (vison interaction potentials, non-Abelian Majorana zero-mode splitting, and local spin/gauge screening clouds), (iii) finite-temperature $T > 0$ states via Fermi-Dirac thermal covariance $\Omega_T = \ii \tanh(\ii \beta \mathbf{A}/2)$ (two-peak specific heat $C_v(T)$, entropy release, and thermal spinodal melting), and (iv) equal-time static spin correlations $S^{\alpha\beta}(\mathbf{q})$.
\item \textbf{Phase 3 (Dynamical Spin Correlations and MZM Spectroscopy -- arXiv:2209.12930):} Full formulation and calculation of dynamical spin correlations $S^{\alpha\beta}(\mathbf{q}, \omega)$ and scanning tunneling spectroscopy (STS) of Majorana zero modes in the pure FM Kitaev $[111]$ model on real-space lattices, benchmarked against exact gauge-quench dynamics (Knolle et al.\ 2014) with strict physical matter-parity selection rules (Pedrocchi et al.\ 2011).
\item \textbf{Phase 4 (Antiferromagnetic Kitaev Model $K > 0$ in $[111]$ Field):} Investigating the intermediate gapless U(1) spin-liquid candidate phase ($h_{c1} < h < h_{c2}$), emergent gauge Fermi surfaces, and neutral spinon thermodynamic signatures.
\item \textbf{Phase 5 (Non-Integrable Material Hamiltonians $J\text{--}K\text{--}\Gamma\text{--}\Gamma'\text{--}D$):} Charting competing zigzag, stripy, and chiral spin-liquid phases relevant to $\alpha\text{-RuCl}_3$ and $\text{Na}_2\text{IrO}_3$.
\item \textbf{Phase 6 (Three-Dimensional Tricoordinated Kitaev Lattices):} Extending Track~B matrix sign purification to 3D hyperhoneycomb and stripyhoneycomb lattices where Majorana Fermi surfaces and nodal loops emerge.
\end{enumerate}
