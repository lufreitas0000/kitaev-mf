# Note 04b: Gaussian Energetics and Double-Counting Test Specification

**Status:** M1.4.3 analytical derivation and test implementation
**Parent note:** [Note 04](note_04_observables_energetics.md)
**Scope:** Derive the scalar subtraction in the quadratic mean-field Hamiltonian, validate the Gaussian energy against finite-Fock-space expectations, and fix pure-Kitaev energy normalization for the Hamiltonian convention of Note 00d.

## 1. Quadratic operator in real space

For one directed physical bond $b=(i,j)$, define
\[
m_i^a=\operatorname{tr}(V_i^TN^a),\qquad
C_b^{ab}=\operatorname{tr}\!\left[(N^a)^TU_bN^bU_b^T\right].
\]
The Bloch construction in Note 02 corresponds in real space to
\[
H_{\mathrm{quad}}=\frac{i}{4}\mathbf c^TM_{\mathrm{real}}\mathbf c,
\]
with on-site blocks
\[
B_i^{(b)}=\sum_{a,b'}J_b^{ab'}N^a m_j^{b'},\qquad
B_j^{(b)}=\sum_{a,b'}J_b^{b'a}N^a m_i^{b'},
\]
and directed hopping block
\[
A_b=2\sum_{a,b'}J_b^{ab'}N^aU_bN^{b'}.
\]
The full real-space skew matrix has blocks $M_{ii}=B_i$, $M_{jj}=B_j$, $M_{ij}=A_b$, and $M_{ji}=-A_b^T$. Each physical edge occurs once.

## 2. Derivation of the scalar double-counting term

The Gaussian expectation of the two on-site contributions generated by one bond is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\left\langle\frac{i}{4}c_i^TB_i^{(b)}c_i
+\frac{i}{4}c_j^TB_j^{(b)}c_j\right\rangle_{\mathrm G}
&=\frac14\sum_{a,b'}J_b^{ab'}
\left(m_i^am_j^{b'}+m_j^{b'}m_i^a\right)\\
&=\frac12\sum_{a,b'}J_b^{ab'}m_i^am_j^{b'}.
\end{aligned}
\]
For the hopping block, the two off-diagonal blocks in $M_{\mathrm{real}}$ combine to give
\[
H_{\mathrm{hop}}^{(b)}
=\frac{i}{2}c_i^TA_bc_j.
\]
Using $U_b^{\mu\nu}=\langle i c_i^\mu c_j^\nu\rangle_{\mathrm G}$ and the skew symmetry of $N^a,N^{b'}$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\langle H_{\mathrm{hop}}^{(b)}\rangle_{\mathrm G}
&=\frac12\sum_{\mu,\nu}(A_b)_{\mu\nu}U_b^{\mu\nu}\\
&=\sum_{a,b'}J_b^{ab'}
\sum_{\mu,\nu}(N^aU_bN^{b'})_{\mu\nu}U_b^{\mu\nu}\\
&=-\sum_{a,b'}J_b^{ab'}C_b^{ab'}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\langle H_{\mathrm{quad}}^{(b)}\rangle_{\mathrm G}
=2\sum_{a,b'}J_b^{ab'}
\left(\frac14m_i^am_j^{b'}-\frac12C_b^{ab'}\right)
=2E_{b,\mathrm{ex}}^{\mathrm G}.
}
\]
The on-site field blocks $-2h_i^aN^a$ contribute exactly the Zeeman expectation
$-\frac12\sum_{i,a}h_i^am_i^a$. The multiplier blocks $\lambda_i^\gamma G^\gamma$ contribute exactly
$-\frac14\sum_{i,\gamma}\lambda_i^\gamma g_i^\gamma$.

Summing over directed physical edges gives
\[
\langle H_{\mathrm{quad}}\rangle_{\mathrm G}
=2E_{\mathrm{ex}}^{\mathrm G}
+E_h^{\mathrm G}+E_\lambda^{\mathrm G}.
\]
Consequently, with the convention used in Notes 02 and 03,
\[
H_{\mathrm{MF}}=H_{\mathrm{quad}}-\mathrm C,
\]
the scalar is
\[
\boxed{
\mathrm C=E_{\mathrm{ex}}^{\mathrm G}
=\sum_{b=(i,j)\in\mathcal E}\sum_{a,b'}J_b^{ab'}
\left[
\frac14m_i^am_j^{b'}
-\frac12\operatorname{tr}\!\left((N^a)^TU_bN^{b'}U_b^T\right)
\right].
}
\]
It is the exchange-channel Gaussian energy only. The Zeeman term and the constraint-multiplier term are already counted once in $H_{\mathrm{quad}}$ and must not be included in $\mathrm C$.

Thus
\[
\boxed{
\langle H_{\mathrm{MF}}\rangle_{\mathrm G}
=E_{\mathrm{ex}}^{\mathrm G}+E_h^{\mathrm G}+E_\lambda^{\mathrm G}
=E_{\mathrm{MF}}^{\mathrm G}[U,V,\lambda].
}
\]
This identity holds when the quadratic blocks are constructed from the same covariances appearing in the Wick functional. It is a finite-dimensional Gaussian identity, not a statement that the interacting spin Hamiltonian equals its quadratic mean-field approximation as an operator.

The derivation fixes the sign in $H_{\mathrm{MF}}=H_{\mathrm{quad}}-\mathrm C$. In particular, for a negative exchange energy, $\mathrm C$ is negative and subtracting it raises the quadratic spectral ground-state energy to the variational energy.

## 3. Pure Kitaev normalization

Use the two-sublattice primitive honeycomb cell, the directed A-to-B convention, $K<0$, and the zero-flux Gaussian covariance
\[
V_s=0,\qquad U_b^{00}=-\bar U,\qquad U_b^{\gamma\gamma}=+1,
\]
with all other entries zero and
\[
\bar U=0.5248645\ldots
\]
as defined by the thermodynamic-limit integral in Note 03. Since $m_i^a=0$, the pure Kitaev exchange contraction on a bond of type $\gamma$ is
\[
C_b^{\gamma\gamma}
=\operatorname{tr}\!\left[(N^\gamma)^TU_bN^\gamma U_b^T\right]
=2U_b^{00}U_b^{\gamma\gamma}
=-2\bar U.
\]
Hence
\[
\langle\sigma_i^\gamma\sigma_j^\gamma\rangle_{\mathrm G}
=-\frac12C_b^{\gamma\gamma}
=-U_b^{00}U_b^{\gamma\gamma}
=\bar U.
\]
For the Hamiltonian convention of Note 00d, in which the coefficient $K$ multiplies the Pauli product $\sigma_i^\gamma\sigma_j^\gamma$ once per physical edge,
\[
E_{\mathrm{bond}}=K\bar U,\qquad
E_{\mathrm{cell}}=3K\bar U,\qquad
E_{\mathrm{site}}=\frac32K\bar U.
\]
At $K=-1$, these are approximately $-0.5248645$, $-1.5745935$, and $-0.78729675$, respectively. These numbers are tied to the Pauli-operator Hamiltonian convention; a Hamiltonian written in terms of physical spins $S^\gamma=\sigma^\gamma/2$ has a factor $1/4$ in each bilinear and therefore a correspondingly rescaled coupling.

As a spectral cross-check, the positive Bloch eigenvalues in Note 02 are
\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{matter}}^+(\mathbf k)
=\frac{|K|}{2}|f(\mathbf k)|,\qquad
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{gauge}}^+(\mathbf k)
=\frac{|K|}{2}\bar U
\]
with three gauge flavors, and $\langle|f|\rangle_{\mathrm{BZ}}=3\bar U$. The full-BZ quadratic ground-state contribution is therefore
\[
E_{\mathrm{quad},0}/N_{\mathrm{cell}}
=-2\left\langle
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{matter}}^+
+3\mathcal E_{\mathrm{gauge}}^+
\right\rangle_{\mathrm{BZ}}
=-6|K|\bar U.
\]
The scalar is $\mathrm C/N_{\mathrm{cell}}=3K\bar U=-3|K|\bar U$, so
\[
(E_{\mathrm{quad},0}-\mathrm C)/N_{\mathrm{cell}}
=-3|K|\bar U
=E_{\mathrm{cell}}.
\]
This is the required spectral-versus-Wick normalization check. The finite-mesh implementation is not expected to reproduce the thermodynamic-limit decimal to arbitrary precision; the analytic identities and mesh-convergence evidence must be reported separately.

## 4. Test contracts

The implementation in test/unit/test_energetics.py uses a deterministic, nondegenerate pure Gaussian state on the two-site, eight-Majorana Fock space. It constructs spin and Majorana operators independently of the production Hamiltonian service.

### T04b-01 — Wick exchange energy versus direct spin expectation

Use a fully populated, nonsymmetric real exchange tensor. Compare the Wick functional for one directed bond against the direct Fock expectation of $\sum_{a,b}J^{ab}\sigma_i^a\sigma_j^b$. This checks tensor ordering and the Hartree/exchange factors without assuming a symmetric exchange tensor.

### T04b-02 — Scalar double-counting identity

Construct the real-space quadratic matrix from the independently specified on-site and hopping blocks. Include nonzero fields and constraint multipliers. Compare its direct Fock expectation with
\[
2E_{\mathrm{ex}}^{\mathrm G}+E_h^{\mathrm G}+E_\lambda^{\mathrm G}.
\]
Then verify that subtracting $\mathrm C=E_{\mathrm{ex}}^{\mathrm G}$ reproduces the full Gaussian variational functional. The test exercises the multiplier residual rather than assuming it vanishes.

### T04b-03 — Pure Kitaev energy units

For the thermodynamic-limit reference covariance, verify the energy per physical bond, per two-site unit cell, and per physical site for $K=-1$ in the Pauli convention. This is an algebraic normalization regression using the stated K-006 decimal; it is not an independent high-precision quadrature calculation.

## 5. Evidence and limitations

T04b-01 and T04b-02 are finite-dimensional numerical evaluations of exact Fock-space identities for a deterministic Gaussian state. They test the algebraic factors and signs to absolute tolerance $3\times10^{-12}$ with zero relative tolerance.

T04b-03 checks arithmetic and unit-cell normalization conditional on the K-006 thermodynamic-limit value. It does not improve the provenance or error bound of that value. A reproducible high-precision quadrature and convergence study remains a separate open task.

No production energetics service is introduced in this batch. The tests deliberately assemble the quadratic operator independently so that M1.4.4 can implement the service against a non-circular oracle.
