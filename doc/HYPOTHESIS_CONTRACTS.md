## 2. Detailed Analysis of Refuted / Failed Hypotheses & Algorithmic Pivots

A core strength of the Living Lab Book methodology is that **failed or refuted hypotheses are preserved and diagnosed mathematically**, preventing silent regressions. Four major hypotheses failed or were refuted during Phase 1, each driving a crucial theoretical or algorithmic breakthrough:

### 2.1 Refutation of `PH-01`: Quadratic vs. Linear Weak-Field Gauge Multiplier Scaling
- **Initial Hypothesis (`m1_6_t0`):** At weak magnetic fields $h \in [0.005, 0.05]$, the converged Lagrange multipliers $\boldsymbol{\lambda}_s^*$ enforcing $\langle \tau_s^\gamma \rangle_{\mathrm{G}} = 0$ were hypothesized to scale quadratically ($\|\boldsymbol{\lambda}^*(h)\|_2 \propto h^2$, expected ratio $\|\boldsymbol{\lambda}^*(0.05)\| / \|\boldsymbol{\lambda}^*(0.01)\| \approx 25.0$), based on the intuition that the pure-Kitaev ground state at $h=0$ has $\boldsymbol{\lambda}^*=0$ and time-reversal symmetry suppresses even/odd parity mixing at first order.
- **Empirical Test & Refutation (`m1_6_t3`):** High-precision Joint DIIS and JFNK solutions at $h = 0.01$ and $h = 0.05$ yielded:
  $$\|\boldsymbol{\lambda}^*(0.01)\|_2 = 2.2389 \times 10^{-2}, \qquad \|\boldsymbol{\lambda}^*(0.05)\|_2 = 1.1238 \times 10^{-1}, \qquad \frac{\|\boldsymbol{\lambda}^*(0.05)\|_2}{\|\boldsymbol{\lambda}^*(0.01)\|_2} = 5.019 \implies p = 1.005 \pm 0.002.$$
- **Mathematical Root-Cause & Pivot:** In the 4-Majorana representation, the Zeeman term $-h^\gamma \sigma_i^\gamma = -\frac{\mathrm{i}}{2} h^\gamma c_i^T \mathrm{N}^\gamma c_i$ couples the itinerant matter Majorana $c_i^0$ directly to the localized gauge Majorana $c_i^\gamma$ via $\mathrm{N}^\gamma = L_{0\gamma}$. Because the unperturbed bond Hamiltonian breaks the local symmetry between $c_i^0$ and $c_i^\gamma$ ($\mathrm{U}_b^{00} = -\bar{U} \approx -0.5249$ vs. $\mathrm{U}_b^{\gamma\gamma} = +1$), a linear Zeeman field induces an immediate $\mathcal{O}(h)$ off-diagonal covariance $\mathrm{V}_s^{0\gamma} \propto h$ and a concomitant $\mathcal{O}(h)$ gauge violation $\delta g_s^\gamma = \frac{1}{2}\epsilon_{\gamma\alpha\beta}\mathrm{V}_s^{\alpha\beta} \propto h$. Restoring the physical gauge-singlet condition $g_s^\gamma = 0$ requires an exact **linear counter-field** $\|\boldsymbol{\lambda}^*(h)\|_2 \approx \sqrt{5} h$ (`K-008`). Crucially, because the multiplier couples to the dual gauge generator $\mathrm{G}^\gamma$ ($\mathrm{G}^\gamma_{\alpha\beta} = -\epsilon_{\gamma\alpha\beta}$), this $\mathcal{O}(h)$ counter-field mixes distinct gauge flavors $c^\alpha \leftrightarrow c^\beta$ and provides the essential intermediate vertex for the $\mathcal{O}(h^3)$ chiral Dirac mass in HFB mean-field theory (`HC-P1-01` / `K-019`).

### 2.2 Failure of `AH-04` (Arm A): Floating-Point Skew-Symmetry Drift in Krylov Subspaces
- **Initial Assumption:** Because the exact analytical fixed-point map $\Phi(\Xi)$ produces skew-symmetric on-site correlation matrices $\mathrm{V}_s^T = -\mathrm{V}_s \in \mathfrak{so}(4)$, an unconstrained numerical accelerator (`Arm A: raw`) should preserve skew-symmetry automatically.
- **Empirical Failure (`m1_6_t2`, `scripts/m1_6/ab_skew_retraction.py`):** While `Arm A` survived in simple Picard and DIIS loops, it **crashed with a fatal `ValueError` at outer step 2 in JFNK (`newton_krylov`)**. Finite-difference directional derivatives $\frac{\mathcal{F}(\mathbf{x} + \epsilon \mathbf{v}) - \mathcal{F}(\mathbf{x})}{\epsilon}$ with $\epsilon \sim 10^{-8}$ amplify IEEE-754 floating-point round-off asymmetry by $\epsilon^{-1} \sim 10^8$, pushing $\|\mathrm{V}_s + \mathrm{V}_s^T\|_\infty > 10^{-12}$ and violating the $\mathfrak{so}(4)$ domain invariant.
- **Resolution:** Enforcing `Arm B` (output antisymmetrization $\mathrm{V}_s \leftarrow \frac{1}{2}(\mathrm{V}_s - \mathrm{V}_s^T)$ inside $\Phi$) and `Arm C` (post-accelerator manifold retraction) extinguishes any injected $10^{-3}$ symmetric perturbation to $0.00 \times 10^0$ in a single step with zero measurable runtime overhead.

### 2.3 Failure of Naive Inner Warm-Starting in Matrix Sign Purification (`C-M1.9-T1`)
- **Initial Hypothesis:** In self-consistent real-space loops $\Omega^{(k)} = \mathrm{i}\operatorname{sgn}(\mathrm{i}\mathbf{A}[\Omega^{(k-1)}])$, initializing the inner Newton-Schulz iteration $\mathbf{Y}_{n+1} = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{Y}_n(3\mathbf{I} + \mathbf{Y}_n^2)$ directly from the previous covariance matrix $\mathbf{Y}_0 = \Omega^{(k-1)}$ would reduce inner iterations from $12$ to $2\text{--}3$.
- **Algebraic Failure Proof (`m1_9_t1`):** Because any pure Grassmannian covariance matrix satisfies $(\Omega^{(k-1)})^2 = -\mathbf{I}$, substituting $\mathbf{Y}_0 = \Omega^{(k-1)}$ into the Newton-Schulz polynomial yields:
  $$\mathbf{Y}_1 = \frac{1}{2}\Omega^{(k-1)}\left(3\mathbf{I} + (\Omega^{(k-1)})^2\right) = \frac{1}{2}\Omega^{(k-1)}(3\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{I}) = \Omega^{(k-1)}.$$
  Moreover, because $\mathbf{Y}_{n+1}$ is a pure polynomial in $\mathbf{Y}_0$, it commutes with $\mathbf{Y}_0$ at every step and **can never rotate the eigenbasis** toward the new Hamiltonian $\mathbf{A}^{(k)}$!
- **Pivot & Resolution (`K-021`):** Warm-starting must either inject the first-order commutator gradient $\mathbf{Y}_0 = \Omega^{(k-1)} - \tau(\mathbf{A}^{(k)} + \Omega^{(k-1)}\mathbf{A}^{(k)}\Omega^{(k-1)})$ (which breaks idempotency along the off-diagonal occupied-virtual block) or operate in the **outer fixed-point accelerator** (Joint DIIS or damped L-Broyden on the variational state $\mathbf{x}^{(k)}$ while initializing the inner sign filter from $\mathbf{Y}_0 = -\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{x}^{(k)}) / \|\mathbf{A}\|_F$). This outer-accelerated purification converges in $7\text{--}9$ steps (DIIS) and $10\text{--}13$ steps (L-Broyden).

### 2.4 Failure of Undamped Unit-Step L-Broyden (`AH-05` $\to$ `H9` / `C-M1.9-T3`)
- **Initial Failure (`m1_6_t10`):** In the 30-trial statistical benchmark at $h=0.20$ and $h=0.40$, Full Broyden achieved an unconditional $90\%$ survival basin radius of $R_{90} \ge 0.50$, whereas the initial Limited-Memory Broyden (`L-Broyden`, $m=10$) collapsed to $R_{90} = 0.010$.
- **Mathematical Diagnosis (`m1_9_t3`):** The L-Broyden inverse Jacobian approximation acts as $H_m \mathbf{v} = -\alpha \mathbf{v} + \sum_{j=0}^{m-1} \mathbf{u}_j (\mathbf{w}_j^T \mathbf{v})$. For any perturbation component $\mathbf{v} \in \operatorname{span}\{\mathbf{s}_0, \dots, \mathbf{s}_{m-1}\}^\perp$ in the $D - m$ unmeasured directions, the update reduces to an unaccelerated Picard step $-\alpha \mathbf{v}$. Setting $\alpha = 1.0$ caused violent oscillatory blowup in the orthogonal complement because unit-step Picard is unstable ($\rho > 1$).
- **Pivot & Validation (`K-013`, `K-021`):** Setting base damping $\alpha = 0.5$ stabilizes the $D - m$ orthogonal complement, expanding the L-Broyden survival basin to $R_{90} = 0.22\text{--}0.35$ and achieving $100\%$ convergence across $h \in [0.01, 0.30]$ in $10\text{--}13$ iterations with strict $\mathcal{O}(mD)$ memory ($1.84\text{ MB}$ vs. $1.06\text{ GB}$ at $D=11{,}520$).

### 3.1 `HC-P1-01` (`H6` / `K-019`): Exact Perturbation vs. Self-Consistent Mean-Field Cubic Gap Prefactor
- **Question:** Why does the self-consistent 4-flavor mean-field Dirac gap $\Delta_K^{\mathrm{MF}}(h) = (2.5921 \pm 0.0034) (h/|K|)^3$ differ numerically from Kitaev's (2006) exact 3rd-order vison-perturbation gap?
- **Exact Quantitative Decomposition (`test_hc_p1_01_perturbative_gap_reconciliation_identities`):**
  1. **Canonical Bogoliubov Factor of 4 (`K-004`):** Our Bloch matrix $\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{k})$ is defined via $H = \sum_{\mathbf{k}} \psi_{\mathbf{k}}^\dagger \mathcal{H}(\mathbf{k}) \psi_{\mathbf{k}}$, yielding physical fermionic quasiparticle excitation energies $\varepsilon_n(\mathbf{k}) = 4\mathcal{E}_n(\mathbf{k})$. In canonical Majorana normalization, the full mean-field gap is:
     $$\Delta_{\mathrm{canonical}}^{\mathrm{MF}}(h) = 4 \Delta_K^{\mathrm{MF}}(h) = 4 \times 2.5921 h^3 = 10.3684 \frac{h^3}{|K|^2}.$$
  2. **Intermediate Excitation Energy Denominator ($15.81\times$):** In third-order spin perturbation theory on the physical subspace, a single spin flip $\sigma_i^\gamma$ creates a pair of $\mathbb{Z}_2$ visons together with dispersive matter Majoranas. In Kitaev's static single-mode approximation (replacing the full two-vison + matter Feshbach resolvent $Q(E_0 - H_K)^{-1}Q$ by the isolated two-vison threshold $\Delta_{\mathrm{flux}} \approx 0.264 |K|$), the reference scale is $\Delta_{\mathrm{static}} = \frac{4}{\Delta_{\mathrm{flux}}^2} h^3 \approx 57.40 h^3/|K|^2$. In unprojected Gaussian mean-field theory, the intermediate virtual state involves localized flat-band gauge Majoranas with two-particle energy $2\Delta_{\mathrm{gauge}} = 2|K|\bar{U} \approx 1.04973 |K|$. The bare squared denominator ratio is:
     $$\left(\frac{2\Delta_{\mathrm{gauge}}}{\Delta_{\mathrm{flux}}}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{1.0497315}{0.2640}\right)^2 = 15.8106.$$
  3. **Self-Consistent KKT Multiplier and Fock Vertex Dressing (`Contract H6`):** Because the bare Zeeman matrix $-\sum_\gamma h_\gamma \mathrm{N}^\gamma$ only couples $c^0 \leftrightarrow c^\gamma$, the $\mathcal{O}(h^3)$ chiral Dirac mass in 4-flavor HFB theory is generated by the self-consistent $\mathcal{O}(h)$ linear response: the KKT multiplier counter-field $\lambda_s^\gamma \mathrm{G}^\gamma$ (mixing $c^\alpha \leftrightarrow c^\beta$) together with the off-diagonal Fock bond self-energy $\delta \mathrm{U}_b = \mathcal{O}(h)$.
